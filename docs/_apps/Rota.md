Rota is an Android app to keep track of how often you wear your clothes, alone or in outfits, so the same few
favorites don't get all the wear. It works entirely on your phone and doesn't even ask for internet access, so your
data can't go anywhere.

Rota started from a clothes spreadsheet: import its tabs once and the app takes over the counting, then export the
counts back whenever you want the sheet up to date.
