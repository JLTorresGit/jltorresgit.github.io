Accounts Manager is an Android app for keeping track of your accounts, deposits, withdrawals and transfers. It keeps
your finances on your phone: there's no account to create and nothing is sent to a server. The app only goes online to
load map tiles from OpenFreeMap when you open a map.

It handles everyday accounts, credit cards, investments and points programs. Scheduled and recurring transactions show
up ahead of time, so you can see where your balances are heading.
