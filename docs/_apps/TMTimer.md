TM Timer keeps time for speakers at Toastmasters meetings. It shows the green, yellow and red signals at the right
moments, on the phone itself or on a Bluetooth light, and keeps a list of the session's speakers and their times.

It works entirely on your phone and doesn't ask for internet access. The only permission it uses is Bluetooth, and
only if you connect a timing light.
