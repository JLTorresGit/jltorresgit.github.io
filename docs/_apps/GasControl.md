Gas Control is an Android app to keep track of fuel loads, vehicle expenses and maintenance for one or more
vehicles. It works entirely on your phone: there's no account to create and nothing is sent to a server.

Add a load in a few taps from the launcher shortcut, and Gas Control works out your consumption between full tanks,
what each kilometer costs you, and when the next oil change is due. Your data stays yours: back it up to a file,
let Android keep an encrypted copy in your Google account, or export it to a spreadsheet whenever you want.
